Uptimer watches your services and tells you, with its reasons, when one is in trouble.

- A **Resource** is something you watch: a website, an API, a job.
- A **Signal** is one stream of evidence about it. A managed worker or your own scripts send **Observations** on it.
- A **Rule** reads the Signals and decides whether the Resource is in trouble.
- An **Incident** is one period of trouble from one Rule. It has a lifecycle (open, closed), a confirmation (confirmed, unconfirmed) and a history.
- A **Workspace** holds Resources and their people.
